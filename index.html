A Soft Start

For days when getting up already feels like a lot.

Low energy3 minutesStay where you are
  1. One slow breath: in for 4, out for 6. That's the whole first step.
  2. Stretch just your fingers and toes. Nothing more is required.
  3. Sip water or something warm before any task asks anything of you.
  4. Name one tiny kind thing you'll do for yourself today. Tiny counts.

Grounding 5-4-3-2-1

Come back to your body when your mind is spinning.

Some energy4 minutesAnywhere
  1. Notice 5 things you can see. Let your eyes rest on each one.
  2. Find 4 things you can touch — fabric, a mug, your own hands.
  3. Listen for 3 sounds. Near or far, soft or loud.
  4. Name 2 things you can smell, and 1 thing you can taste. You're here.

Tend & Glow

A full, gentle routine for the days you have room for it.

Good energy10 minutesMature-skin friendly
  1. Cleanse with a cream or balm cleanser — skip harsh foaming washes.
  2. Hydrate: a few drops of hyaluronic serum on damp skin.
  3. Nourish: a rich, fragrance-free moisturizer or facial oil, massaged in slow upward strokes.
  4. Protect: SPF 30+ — the single best thing you can do for mature skin.

Skipping a step is not failing. Your skin forgives you.
